# Twilight workspace

Interviewed direction, with implementation choices labeled below. This refines the existing Next.js portfolio, not a separate marketing site.

## The eight topics

1. **Mood.** User: “feeling of melancholy and feeling lost”. No new reference was supplied. Existing evidence: Catppuccin Mocha/Latte, pixel stars, anime portraits, Linux/Waybar, Lily Chou-Chou, music and cinema.
2. **Journey.** Previously approved: “Keep the identity; surface projects earlier”. Retain introduction → selected projects → GitHub/tools/experience → interests → contact. Hiring target: “Internships primary; junior roles welcome”.
3. **Energy.** Implementation assumption: quiet opening, concentrated project evidence, a short typographic release, calm credentials, intimate interests, settled close. No long pinning or scroll delays.
4. **Remembered feeling.** User: “my overall personality”. Implementation: let portraits, real work, Linux, music and cinema accumulate into one person rather than introduce a separate spectacle.
5. **Signature.** Implementation choice: a contact-sheet portrait stack. Two real existing alternate profiles sit behind the active portrait, separate slightly on scroll, and rotate through the existing portrait button. The same person can be seen through different interests, without fabricated identities or telemetry.
6. **Range.** User requests “premium” with “my personalities intact”. Interpretation: restrained nocturnal personal workspace, with illustrated details from the existing identity. Preserve both Catppuccin themes and the existing typefaces.
7. **Structure.** User: “Distinct scenes in my personal workspace”. Use natural document sections and working workspace navigation. No continuous world, forced horizontal travel, or scroll hijacking.
8. **Assets.** Existing portraits, Reze illustration, real screenshots, OwlSet app video, pixel-star canvas and GitHub history. Reuse these. No image generation, new external assets, paid API, or new dependencies.

## Feeling curve (before the score)

- Opening: wistful curiosity. Personal photos sit in a layered contact sheet against a distant, quiet twilight field.
- Projects: confidence, the emotional peak. Real interfaces come into focus and their controls expose actual work; this collection occupies the largest useful part of the page.
- Interlude: release. “Build for people. Make it personal.” travels gently across the page.
- Evidence: trust. Honest contribution history, skills and biographical details settle into plain, readable rows.
- Interests: intimacy. Music, film, Linux and the existing Japanese twilight motif form a slower personal scene.
- Contact: resolve. A held, complete invitation and a real email address finish the page.

## Peak

“The portraits felt like someone’s personal world, and then I could actually explore what they built.” The selected-project collection is the peak and owns the most scroll room through content, not spacers. The quiet hero prepares it.

## Tell-someone sentence

“It’s the site where a quiet stack of personal portraits opens into the things he builds and the things he loves.”

## Silence

Whitespace separates the scenes. No blank viewport, empty pinning, or loading-dependent pause is intentional. Essential copy and controls are always available with motion disabled or JavaScript unavailable.

## Visitor outcome

Audience: hiring teams considering full-stack interns, with junior roles welcome. Belief: Jose can build useful applications and has a distinct personal perspective. Next action: Contact me. Existing project previews, résumé download, and mail/copy controls remain operational.
